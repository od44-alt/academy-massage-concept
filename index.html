Massage therapy

Our registered massage therapists combine techniques to reduce stress, ease pain and help you move better. Book any available therapist, or ask for one you've seen before.

  • Swedish Long, flowing strokes. The usual start if you're new to massage.
  • Deep tissue Slower, deeper pressure for tight necks, low backs, shoulders and legs.
  • Hot stone Heated stones to warm and relax muscles.
  • Couples Two tables, two therapists, one room.
  • Pregnancy Prenatal and postnatal massage.
  • Sports Performance, maintenance and post-event.
  • East Indian head massage On its own or added to a massage.

  • Acupuncture & TCM

    Traditional Chinese medicine with Dr. Wei Zou and Michaud Desilets. Start with an initial consult. Treatments can include acupressure, cupping and acupotomy.

  • Manual osteopathy

    Joint mobilization, muscle energy and soft tissue techniques. Sessions run 50–60 minutes. No referral needed.

  • Infrared laser, sauna & steam

    Near-infrared laser for chronic pain, with a free consultation first. Add a 30-minute infrared sauna or steam shower to any visit.

Direct billing

We submit claims straight to your insurer, at no extra charge.

  1. Before your first visit call us at least 48 hours ahead, or arrive 20 minutes early, so your plan details are on file.
  2. Check your plan. Coverage varies. Some plans need a doctor's note for massage.
  3. We bill, you pay any balance. If we can't bill this time, pay up front and claim it yourself. We'll set you up for next time.

Payment by your insurer is not guaranteed. Your plan is between you and your insurer.

Questions

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations and no-shows are charged 50% of the appointment, and that fee can't be claimed on insurance. Our therapists are paid per client, so notice lets us fill the time.

Are your therapists registered?

Yes. All of our massage therapists are registered massage therapists (RMTs) and can issue receipts for insurance.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

Not unless your insurance plan requires one. If you take certain medications or have a health concern, check with your health care provider first.

What should I wear?

Nothing special. For most treatments you undress to your comfort level under a sheet and blanket. You never remove lower undergarments.

How often should I come in?

That depends on your goals. Your therapist can suggest a plan, including stretches or exercises for home.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Buy a gift card online.

What changed and why

Notes from AI-Lan Consulting for the clinic owner. This section would not appear on the real site.

  1. Phone and Book are always on screen. On the current site, a phone visitor sees a photo and one button. The number and hours are at the bottom of the page.
  2. It loads fast on a phone. The current home page downloads about 39 MB on mobile, mostly the background video, and scored 40/100 on Google's speed test. This page is a small fraction of that.
  3. Price before booking. The finder shows the cost and add-ons, then opens the right booking page.
  4. Direct billing explained in three steps, on the home page, since it's the clinic's biggest selling point.
  5. Google can read the clinic's details. The current site tells Google the business is called "561 Academy Road" with no postal code. This version fixes that.
  6. Health history online, not a printable PDF, plus a privacy page, which the current site does not have.
  7. One price per service. Manual osteopathy shows $150 on one page and $159 on another.
